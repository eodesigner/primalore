## Purpose

To check you have the basics for a day on the hills, and enough to stay safe if the day goes wrong.

## Who it is for

Walkers and scramblers on UK hills. Climbing and winter mountaineering need more specialist kit.

## Checklist

**Clothing**
- [ ] Footwear with a good tread that supports your ankles
- [ ] Warm, windproof and waterproof layers, ideally bright
- [ ] Spare clothing, a hat and gloves, even in summer

**Food and drink**
- [ ] Plenty of food, including quick-energy snacks
- [ ] Plenty of water

**Navigation and communication**
- [ ] Paper map and compass, within easy reach
- [ ] Mobile phone, fully charged (a power pack if you can)
- [ ] Phone registered with emergency SMS if you may need it
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Torch, plus a spare head torch and spare batteries
- [ ] A watch for at least one person in the group

**Safety**
- [ ] First aid kit and any essential personal medication
- [ ] Emergency layer or bivvy bag (cold sets in quickly at height)
- [ ] Helmet if climbing or mountain biking
- [ ] Route note left with someone reliable

## When to review

Before each trip. Check your torch batteries and first aid kit, and recharge your phone and power pack.
