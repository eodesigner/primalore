## Purpose

A short note left with someone reliable lets them raise the alarm if you do not return, and tells rescuers where to begin.

## Who it is for

Anyone heading into the hills, caves or other remote ground, including solo walkers, who carry the highest risk because nobody else is there to help.

## Checklist

- [ ] Where you will start and where you plan to finish
- [ ] The route you intend to take
- [ ] Alternative routes if the weather or tiredness forces a change
- [ ] The time you expect to be back
- [ ] Your contact details and those of everyone in the party
- [ ] The note is with a reliable person or left in a place they will find it
- [ ] You have told them what to do and whom to call if you are overdue
- [ ] You stick to the plan, and tell them promptly if it changes
- [ ] You tell them when you are safely back

## When to review

Write a fresh note for every trip. Update it when your plans change on the day.
