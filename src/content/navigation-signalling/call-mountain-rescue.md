## Question

Who do I call when someone is hurt or lost on a hill, and what do I say?

## Answer

Dial 999 or 112. Ask for the **police** first. Once you are through, ask for **mountain rescue** (or cave rescue underground). The police pass the call on to the rescue team.

Have these ready before you speak:

- Your location, ideally a grid reference
- The casualty's name, sex and age
- What happened and what the injuries or problem are
- How many people are in your party
- Your mobile number, in case the call drops

If you need to call again, go through the same route.

## Deaf or speech-impaired callers

Register with the emergency SMS service in advance by texting "register" to 999 and following the reply. After that you can text 999 in an emergency.

## Caution

Mountain areas often have patchy signal. If the call will not connect, try moving a short distance only if it is safe, or use a whistle signal and stay put.
