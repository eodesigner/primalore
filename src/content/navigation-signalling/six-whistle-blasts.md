## Question

How do I use a whistle to call for help when I cannot reach anyone by phone?

## Answer

Give six long blasts in about a minute. Stop for a minute. Then repeat the pattern. A torch can send the same pattern in the dark: six long flashes, a minute's rest, repeat.

Keep going until rescuers physically reach you. If you hear a reply, do not stop. The people looking for you may be using your signal to find your direction.

## Caution

Signalling does not replace a phone call. If you have a signal, call 999 or 112 first and ask for the police, then mountain rescue. Stay where you are once you have signalled, so searchers know where to look.
