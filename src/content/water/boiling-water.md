## Question

How long should water boil before you drink it?

## Answer

Bring clear water to a rolling boil and keep it there for **one minute**. Above roughly 6,500 feet (about 2,000 metres), boil it for **three minutes**. Let it cool in the covered pot or a clean, lidded container.

## Caution

Boiling kills germs but does not remove chemicals, fuel, or heavy metals. Cloudy water should be left to settle and strained through a clean cloth before boiling. If the water may be contaminated with chemicals, do not drink it; find another source.
