## Question

What is the quickest way to fasten a rope to a pole or rail, and adjust it later?

## Answer

Use a clove hitch. It takes two turns around the anchor, with the second turn trapped under a crossing strand.

**At the end of a rope**

1. Make one turn around the anchor.
2. Bring the rope over the standing part and down, forming a diagonal bar across the first turn.
3. Make a second turn around the anchor.
4. Tuck the working end under the diagonal bar.
5. Pull both ends to dress the knot. The bar should lie opposite to the direction of pull.

**In the middle of a rope**

1. Hold the rope in both hands about 30 cm (12 inches) apart.
2. Form two loops, one toward each hand, and lay one loop on top of the other without flipping it.
3. Drop both loops over the anchor and pull the ends in opposite directions to tighten.

## Caution

The Army guide rates the clove hitch at roughly 60 to 65 per cent of rope strength. It depends on steady tension to stay put, so check it regularly and do not trust it with a job that matters without a safety knot. Do not use it for a load that supports a person without instruction.
