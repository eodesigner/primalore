## Question

How do I join two ropes that are the same thickness?

## Answer

1. Lay the two rope ends across each other, right end over left.
2. Wrap the right end under and back over the top, as in the first half of tying a shoelace.
3. Reverse the pattern: now pass the left end over the right, then under and back across.
4. Pull the standing parts to tighten and dress the knot. The two working ends should come out on the same side of their own standing rope, one on each side of the knot.
5. Tie an overhand knot with each working end around its own standing part as a safety, leaving a tail of at least 10 cm (4 inches).

## Caution

The Army guide uses the square knot for ropes of equal diameter. For ropes of different thickness a figure-eight bend (a different knot, with its own limits) is the guide's choice for diameters within 5 mm of each other. Knots can loosen over time, so check them and re-tie when needed. Never trust any knot with a person's weight until you have been taught by a qualified instructor.
